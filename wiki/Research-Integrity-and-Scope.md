# Research Integrity and Scope

[Live prototype](https://bdcmr.ai-aarti.com) · [[Home]] · [Creator: Aarti S Ravikumar](https://ai-aarti.com)

## The central boundary

BD-CMR is a browser-based research and education prototype. It uses authored synthetic scenarios, procedural anatomy, and illustrative mathematical models. It is **not** a medical device, clinical information system, diagnostic classifier, therapeutic controller, or source of patient-specific advice.

## What the prototype demonstrates

- Transparent representation of simulated evidence and uncertainty.
- Role-aware research workspaces with presentation boundaries.
- Deterministic scenario evaluation and watermarked research exports.
- Review-gated anatomy and explicit unknown states.
- A documented pathway for future governed clinical-research work.

## What it does not demonstrate

- Myocardial regeneration, therapeutic efficacy, or safety.
- Patient-specific anatomy, validated measurements, diagnosis, prognosis, or treatment selection.
- Real EHR, FHIR, DICOM, PHI, PACS, or production identity integration.
- Clinical authorization, regulatory clearance, or medical-device validation.

## How to interpret the interface

Treat visual confidence as a prompt to inspect evidence—not as evidence itself. A value or graphic may be authored, simulated, inferred, reviewed, or unknown. The interface is designed to keep those states visible.
