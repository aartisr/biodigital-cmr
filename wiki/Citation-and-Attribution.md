## Citation guidance

When discussing the project, identify it as a **research prototype** and preserve its limitations. Do not describe it as a clinically validated system, medical device, treatment, diagnostic method, or evidence of myocardial regeneration.

For release-specific reference, cite the release date and the public project URL. The public site is the preferred destination for general readers: [https://bdcmr.ai-aarti.com](https://bdcmr.ai-aarti.com).
