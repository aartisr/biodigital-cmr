# BD-CMR Wiki

> **BD-CMR is an open research prototype for making cardiovascular simulations, uncertainty, provenance, and safety boundaries easier to inspect.**

[Open the live research prototype](https://bdcmr.ai-aarti.com) · [Meet the creator, Aarti S Ravikumar](https://ai-aarti.com)

## Why this project exists

Biomedical interfaces can look more certain than the evidence behind them. BD-CMR explores a different premise: sophisticated visualizations should reveal assumptions, evidence gaps, provenance, and limits instead of concealing them.

The project is designed for research, education, and critical discussion—not clinical decision-making.

## Core principles

1. **Evidence before confidence.** Simulated, inferred, reviewed, and unknown states must remain distinguishable.
2. **Safety by visible boundaries.** A compelling interface must not be mistaken for a medical device or treatment recommendation.
3. **Provenance is part of the result.** Every research artifact should retain its source state, limitations, and version context.
4. **Human review is indispensable.** No model or visualization replaces expert judgment.
5. **Accessibility and equity are engineering requirements.** Useful research tools must be understandable and usable by the communities they serve.
